Our Story

Our Foundation

McIlhinney Sport Management was founded in 2023 by Sean McIlhinney after 27 years of practicing law, along with his two nephews, Kane Siebold and McKay Siebold, to fufill a lifelong dream to be a NFL sports agent. Sean quickly passed the NFL agent exam and secured his certification and license in 2024. From the agency's inception, we have had a singular vision: to provide unparalleled representation and strategic guidance to college and NFL football players in the ever-evolving business landscape of football. Player First Always.

What started as a passion project has grown into a trusted name in the industry, known for integrity, hard work, innovation, and results. Kane and McKay, both accomplished football players in their own right, are now working full time for the agency and attending law school to become attorneys and licensed agents.

Our journey began with a deep understanding that success in sports management requires more than just business acumen, it demands a genuine passion for the game, hard work, an unwavering commitment to our clients, and the ability to navigate complex challenges with expertise and grace.

Our Mission

At Mcilhinney Sports Management, we believe that every athlete deserves a partner who understands their unique journey and is committed to maximizing their potential both on and off the field. Our mission is to provide comprehensive, personalized management services that empower our clients to achieve their goals and build lasting legacies. What separates us from our competeition is that we are a boutique firm so that we can give special personalized serveice and attention to our clients. Player First Always.

"We don't just manage careers—we build futures. Every decision we make, every strategy we develop, is centered on creating long-term value and success for our clients."

Looking Forward

As the sports industry continues to evolve with new opportunities in NIL and digital media, Mcilhinney Sports Management remains at the forefront of innovation. We're constantly developing and implementing strategies that help our clients thrive in an increasingly competitive landscape. Our commitment to excellence and our passion for sports drive us forward every day to best navigate the complex world of NIL and the NFL and find the most advantageous opportunities for our athletes. Our Team

Expert Leadership

Our team brings decades of combined experience in sports management, contract negotiations, and athlete representation

Sean Mcilhinney

NFL & NIL Agent

Sean is a fully licensed NFL agent which he earned in 2024 and has been a practicing attorney for 30 plus years. Sean graduated cum laude from North Carolina State University in 1991 with a BA in communications and political science. Sean then graduated with honors from Georgia State University College of Law in 1995 where he sat on school's prestigious law review. After graduating from law school, Sean was an attorney at King & Spalding, the premier law firm in the Southeast, where he worked in the products liability division representing Fortune 500 companies. He loved working at King & Spalding but decided to leave the large law firm after three years to open his own small boutique law firm focusing on criminal defense and personal injury. In 2008, Sean was recognized in Georgia Trend magazine as one of Georgia’s elite attorneys for criminal defense. In 2023, Sean founded McIlhinney Sport Management to pursue his life long dream of being a sports agent. From its inception, the agency's focus has been to provide high quality representation and personalized service in the NIL and NFL space. Sean has always had a passion for college and professional football since he was a young boy. He was a champion high school miler placing third in the 1987 NJ State Championship Group 4 (large schools). He is married with 5 children. Sean is known for excellent communication skills, straight forward honesty, loyalty, hard work and strategic thinking.

McKay Siebold

Player Manager & Recruiter

Graduated from Florida State University in 2022 with a degree in Sport Management. Currently McKay is attending Atlanta's John Marshall law school and serves as a recruiter and player manager on the staff. McKay is known for his extensive NFL player knowledge and salary cap intel. McKay was a stand-out wide reciever on his high school football team. McKay holds a MLBPA limited agent certification and plans to secure his NFL agent liscense upon finishing law school.
