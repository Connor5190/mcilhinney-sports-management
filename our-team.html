Our team brings decades of combined experience in sports management, contract negotiations, and athlete representation

Sean Mcilhinney

NFL & NIL Agent

Sean is a fully licensed NFL agent which he earned in 2024 and has been a practicing attorney for 30 plus years. Sean graduated cum laude from North Carolina State University in 1991 with a BA in communications and political science. Sean then graduated with honors from Georgia State University College of Law in 1995 where he sat on school's prestigious law review. After graduating from law school, Sean was an attorney at King & Spalding, the premier law firm in the Southeast, where he worked in the products liability division representing Fortune 500 companies. He loved working at King & Spalding but decided to leave the large law firm after three years to open his own small boutique law firm focusing on criminal defense and personal injury. In 2008, Sean was recognized in Georgia Trend magazine as one of Georgia’s elite attorneys for criminal defense. In 2023, Sean founded McIlhinney Sport Management to pursue his life long dream of being a sports agent. From its inception, the agency's focus has been to provide high quality representation and personalized service in the NIL and NFL space. Sean has always had a passion for college and professional football since he was a young boy. He was a champion high school miler placing third in the 1987 NJ State Championship Group 4 (large schools). He is married with 5 children. Sean is known for excellent communication skills, straight forward honesty, loyalty, hard work and strategic thinking.
