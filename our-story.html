Our Story

Player First Always — the people, mission, and vision behind Mcilhinney Sports Management

Our Foundation

McIlhinney Sport Management was founded in 2023 by Sean McIlhinney after 27 years of practicing law, along with his two nephews, Kane Siebold and McKay Siebold, to fufill a lifelong dream to be a NFL sports agent. Sean quickly passed the NFL agent exam and secured his certification and license in 2024. From the agency's inception, we have had a singular vision: to provide unparalleled representation and strategic guidance to college and NFL football players in the ever-evolving business landscape of football. Player First Always.

What started as a passion project has grown into a trusted name in the industry, known for integrity, hard work, innovation, and results. Kane and McKay, both accomplished football players in their own right, are now working full time for the agency and attending law school to become attorneys and licensed agents.

Our journey began with a deep understanding that success in sports management requires more than just business acumen, it demands a genuine passion for the game, hard work, an unwavering commitment to our clients, and the ability to navigate complex challenges with expertise and grace.

Our Mission

At Mcilhinney Sports Management, we believe that every athlete deserves a partner who understands their unique journey and is committed to maximizing their potential both on and off the field. Our mission is to provide comprehensive, personalized management services that empower our clients to achieve their goals and build lasting legacies. What separates us from our competeition is that we are a boutique firm so that we can give special personalized serveice and attention to our clients. Player First Always.

"We don't just manage careers—we build futures. Every decision we make, every strategy we develop, is centered on creating long-term value and success for our clients."

Looking Forward

As the sports industry continues to evolve with new opportunities in NIL and digital media, Mcilhinney Sports Management remains at the forefront of innovation. We're constantly developing and implementing strategies that help our clients thrive in an increasingly competitive landscape. Our commitment to excellence and our passion for sports drive us forward every day to best navigate the complex world of NIL and the NFL and find the most advantageous opportunities for our athletes.
